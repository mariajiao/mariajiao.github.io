Overview

Working as an Embedded Software and Hardware Engineer on Duke Electric Vehicles team, developing custom CAN bus firmware and PCB designs for the Shell Eco-marathon competition vehicle.

Key Responsibilities

  • Develop custom CAN bus firmware in C++ to manage robust serial communication between distributed microcontrollers across the vehicle
  • Design PCBs for communication modules, interfacing with sensors and subsystems for real-time telemetry and data analytics
  • Research and select low-power, efficient components to reduce energy consumption in peripheral systems
  • Lead new-member onboarding process and technical training sessions
